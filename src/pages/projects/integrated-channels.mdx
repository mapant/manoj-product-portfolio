    <section class="slide white" id="challenge"><div class="wrap">
      <div class="section-head"><div><span class="eyebrow">01 · Product Context</span><h2>The problem was bigger than building 12 applications</h2></div><p>Banks already operated within complex technology environments. The product therefore had to support domain-specific workflows while remaining configurable, integration-ready, migration-aware, controlled and implementable at client sites.</p></div>
      <div class="cards-2">
        <article class="card"><span class="eyebrow">The product challenge</span><h3>One platform had to serve very different banking jobs.</h3><p>Lending, AML, cards, reconciliation, treasury and audit do not share the same users or workflows. The challenge was to find reusable platform patterns without flattening domain complexity.</p><ul><li>Existing bank applications made coexistence and integration critical.</li><li>Client-specific workflows required configuration rather than rigid product forks.</li><li>Regulated processes required roles, approvals, evidence and traceability.</li><li>Legacy data required mapping, transformation, validation and post-migration checks.</li><li>Deployment required training, documentation and L1/L2 readiness.</li></ul></article>
        <article class="card"><span class="eyebrow">Product thesis</span><h3>Standardise the platform. Configure the institution. Integrate the ecosystem.</h3><p>The suite was approached as a common product foundation with modular domain applications — not as 12 disconnected projects.</p><div class="four"><div><b>Standardise</b><span>Roles, controls, workflow patterns and reporting.</span></div><div><b>Configure</b><span>Institution-specific rules and journeys.</span></div><div><b>Integrate</b><span>Bank systems, rails and external services.</span></div><div><b>Adopt</b><span>Migration, deployment, training and support.</span></div></div></article>
      </div>
    </div></section>

    <section class="slide section" id="strategy"><div class="wrap">
      <div class="section-head"><div><span class="eyebrow">02 · Product Strategy</span><h2>From application list to a reusable banking product ecosystem</h2></div><p>The strategy connected platform architecture, domain product design, implementation realities and client adoption. Each decision was intended to reduce repeated effort while preserving the flexibility banks needed.</p></div>
      <div class="strategy-grid">
        <div class="strategy-card" style="--tone:#9b8cf5"><h3>01 · Common platform foundation</h3><p>Define reusable roles, permissions, workflow states, validations, reporting patterns and auditability before multiplying application complexity.</p></div>
        <div class="strategy-card" style="--tone:#73cbb4"><h3>02 · Configurable institution model</h3><p>Separate reusable product behaviour from bank-specific configuration so workflows can adapt without creating a new product.</p></div>
        <div class="strategy-card" style="--tone:#7eb5ed"><h3>03 · Integration-first design</h3><p>Identify systems of record, API contracts, authentication, failures, retries, reconciliation and hand-offs while journeys are designed.</p></div>
        <div class="strategy-card" style="--tone:#efb785"><h3>04 · Controls by design</h3><p>Translate business and regulatory requirements into roles, maker-checker patterns, approvals, validations, evidence and reports.</p></div>
        <div class="strategy-card" style="--tone:#f49dbd"><h3>05 · Migration-aware design</h3><p>Treat existing data as part of the product problem: assess, map, transform, validate, migrate and verify.</p></div>
        <div class="strategy-card" style="--tone:#9dcbed"><h3>06 · Two-phase roadmap</h3><p>Build the first six applications in the initial six months, then sequence the remaining six using phase-one foundations and learning.</p></div>
        <div class="strategy-card" style="--tone:#a8d7a8"><h3>07 · Feedback-led refinement</h3><p>Use demos, bank feedback, QA and UAT to refine workflows, reports, exceptions and implementation readiness.</p></div>
        <div class="strategy-card" style="--tone:#b6a7f0"><h3>08 · Adoption as outcome</h3><p>Carry ownership through manuals, training, L1/L2 enablement, deployment readiness and the enhancement backlog.</p></div>
