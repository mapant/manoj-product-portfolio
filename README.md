# 🩺 02 — Ayu DocConnect

> **Turning doctor relationships into a connected digital care journey.**

Ayu DocConnect is a doctor-engagement and referral platform designed to connect referral doctors, patients, hospitals and healthcare services through a measurable digital experience.

### 🔗 Product Ecosystem

- Referral Doctors
- Patients
- BDMs / Sales
- Ayu Hospitals / Operations
- Growth / Product
- External Healthcare Services

### 🚀 Product Capabilities

| Capability | Product Intent |
|---|---|
| **Patient Referrals** | Enable digital referral creation and management |
| **Patient Tracking** | Create visibility across OPD, IPD and discharge |
| **Incentives & Earnings** | Support referral rewards and earnings visibility |
| **Doctor Network** | Enable doctor-to-doctor discovery and collaboration |
| **Ambulance Services** | Integrate access to healthcare-support services |
| **Google Business** | Extend practice visibility and digital presence |

### 📈 Doctor & Referral Measurement Framework

The product is measured through signals such as:

- **Doctor Activation**
- **Active Doctor Usage**
- **Repeat Referral Rate**
- **Referral Conversion**
- **Journey Visibility**
- **Payout Turnaround**
- **Doctor Lifecycle & Retention**
- **Workflow Adoption & Operational Health**

### 🏆 Pilot Outcomes

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Doctors installed / logged in | **1,150+** |
| Monthly Active Users | **400+** |
| Weekly Active Users | **170+** |
| Doctor referrals in pilot | **27+** |
| Newly onboarded doctors through referral flow | **7** |

### 💡 Product Value

Ayu DocConnect demonstrates how an offline doctor-relationship model can evolve into a measurable digital ecosystem for **referrals, engagement, care-journey visibility and network growth**.

# 📍 03 — Sales Intelligence & Field Force Optimization

> **Helping field-sales teams identify the right prospects, plan smarter visits and execute territories with measurable visibility.**

This product focuses on improving how BDMs and field-sales teams discover prospects, prioritize opportunities, plan visits and optimize daily execution.

### ⚠️ Problem Space

The solution addresses challenges such as:

- poor prospect discovery and quality
- unstructured field visit planning
- excessive travel
- manual referral-agent movement
- limited manager visibility
- inefficient territory utilization
- slower network expansion

### 🧠 Core Product Capabilities

- **Prospect Intelligence & Assignment**
- **Beat Plan & Visit Prioritisation**
- **Agent & Prospect Visit Management**
- **Location & Travel Intelligence**
- **Referral-Agent Movement**
- **Sales Insights & MIS**
- **Agent Lifecycle & Verification**

### 📊 Product Measurement

The measurement framework covers:

- Prospect Quality & Acquisition
- Field Coverage
- Visit Efficiency
- Conversion
- Field Productivity
- Product Adoption & Usage

### 📈 Pilot Outcomes

The Beat Plan pilot demonstrated measurable improvement in field execution:

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---:|
| Increase in unique doctor-agent visits — BDM 1 | **60%** |
| Increase in unique doctor-agent visits — BDM 2 | **166%** |
| Reduction in distance travelled per visit — BDM 1 | **18%** |
| Reduction in distance travelled per visit — BDM 2 | **15%** |

### 💡 Product Value

The case study demonstrates how **product design + analytics + location intelligence + field workflows** can improve sales productivity, territory effectiveness and manager visibility.

# 🧠 My Product Management Approach

<div align="center">

### **Discover → Define → Prioritize → Design → Deliver → Measure → Improve → Scale**

</div>

### 🔎 Discover
Understand customers, business problems, operational friction, workflows and measurable outcomes.

### 🧭 Define
Translate ambiguity into clear product opportunities, use cases, personas, requirements and success measures.

### 🗺️ Prioritize
Balance customer value, business impact, dependencies, effort, regulatory criticality and delivery constraints.

### 🎨 Design
Shape workflows, journeys, product behavior, integrations, data requirements and user experience.

### 🚀 Deliver
Work across Product, Engineering, Design, QA, Analytics, Business, Operations and Compliance to move from requirements to production.

### 📊 Measure
Define signals across adoption, engagement, conversion, workflow success, efficiency, reliability and business impact.

### 🔁 Improve
Use product data, customer feedback, stakeholder input and operational learning to continuously improve the product.

### 📈 Scale
Build processes, product foundations and operating models that support adoption across teams, clients and markets.
